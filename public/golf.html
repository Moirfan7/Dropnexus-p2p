⛳ How to Play

Super Mini Golf Endless Edition

🏌️
Stick Drag & Aim: Click/Touch the ball or screen and drag backwards. The golf stick pulls back and aims automatically.
⚡
Speed Tracker (Power Meter):
• Drag thoda sa: 🟢 LOW symbol.
• Drag medium: 🟡 MED symbol.
• Drag zyada: 🔴 HIGH / MAX symbol with sparks!
💥
Release to Kick: Jaise hi mouse ya finger release karenge, stick aage aakar ball ko kick karegi!
🎯
Multi-Putt System: Agar ball hole me nahi gayi, to ball jahan rukegi, stick wahi wapas aa jayegi. Wahi se dobara drag karke hole me daalein!
♾️
Endless Levels: Har level naye obstacles, sand traps, bouncers aur corridors ke saath infinite endless generate hote hain!
